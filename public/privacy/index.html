Official Privacy Policy

DOŪR (دور) - Language Party Game

Package ID: com.solonovate.dour • Developer: Solonovate • Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how DOŪR collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and related web services.

1. Overview & Commitment

DOŪR (دور) is an interactive vocabulary party game designed to make learning languages fun, dynamic, and educational. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This application is built with a privacy-first approach: we collect only the minimal data strictly necessary to provide the game experience, user accounts, and multiplayer functionality.

Privacy Highlight: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Audio & Microphone Access (Optional / On-Device)

DOŪR offers single-player speech pronunciation challenges. To enable this, the app requests access to your microphone:

  • Microphone input is processed locally in real-time using on-device Web Speech recognition.
  • No audio recordings or voice files are stored, transmitted, or uploaded to our servers.
  • Microphone permission is strictly optional; all other game modes work completely without microphone access.

B. Optional Account Information (Google Sign-In)

Users may choose to sign in using Google Authentication via Google Firebase to save game records, sync progress, and participate in global leaderboards:

  • Data received: Public display name, email address, and profile photo URL provided by Google.
  • Purpose: Creating and identifying your player profile and displaying your score on community leaderboards.
  • Account creation is entirely optional. You can play all core game modes anonymously without logging in.

C. Gameplay and Score Data

When you play, game history records (team scores, rounds played, words guessed, and timestamps) are saved locally on your device (using LocalStorage). If you are signed in, these scores are backed up to our cloud database (Google Cloud Firestore).

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate the local and online multiplayer room features.
  • To display match results, performance statistics, and leaderboards.
  • To retain language learning progress and customize card categories according to your preferences.
  • To detect bugs, prevent abusive behavior, and ensure service reliability.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We utilize trusted cloud infrastructure to securely run our application:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Provides secure authentication and cloud database services. Hosted by Google LLC under enterprise-grade security standards. View Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Facilitates secure app distribution and update management on Android devices.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have complete control over your personal data. We provide two easy methods to permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data:

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion
1. Open the DOŪR app on your device.
2. Tap your Profile Icon in the top header.
3. Navigate to the Data & Privacy tab.
4. Tap the red button: "Delete Account & Cloud Data" and confirm.
All your match histories, user credentials, and cloud records are permanently erased immediately.
Method 2: Web Deletion Request
If you no longer have the app installed, you can submit an account deletion request by emailing us at m.jaberi1212@gmail.com with the subject "DOŪR Account Deletion Request" from your registered Google email address. We will permanently delete all records within 48 hours.
  • Local Data: You can wipe all local scores, settings, and cached game data at any time by clearing your browser or app storage in Android device settings.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

DOŪR is a family-friendly educational game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data without parental approval, we will promptly delete that information from our records.

7. Security Safeguards

We enforce rigorous security measures to protect your information:

  • All network transmissions are strictly encrypted using HTTPS / TLS with valid SSL certificates.
  • Cloud database access is protected by granular Firebase Security Rules that verify user ownership prior to reading or writing records.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published immediately on this publicly accessible webpage with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
