"Dour: Language Party Game" turns language practice into an energetic, laughter-filled social game for parties, game nights, classrooms, and family gatherings. Pass a single phone around the table and help your teammates guess words and real-life phrases before the clock runs out!

★ 4 EXCITING GAME MODES:
1. Team Party (4, 6, or 8 Players): Pass one phone around the circle. Teammates sit opposite each other and take turns giving clues in their support language or target language.
2. 2-Player Screen Duel: Split-screen head-to-head word battle on a single device or across two phones.
3. Solo Practice & Voice Recognition: Practice flashcards alone, speak answers aloud into the microphone with instant speech evaluation, and adjust audio playback speed (1.0x / 0.7x).
4. Leitner Spaced-Repetition Box: Smart 3-box review system (1, 3, and 7-day intervals) ensuring difficult cards stay fresh in your long-term memory.
